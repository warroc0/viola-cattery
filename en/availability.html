How does placement work?

  1. Get in touch. Email, call or message us (06-42291077). Tell us a little about yourself, your home situation and what you are looking for in a kitten.
  2. We get back to you. We’ll let you know whether a kitten is available or when we expect one.
  3. Getting acquainted. You are warmly welcome at our home, over a slice of vlaai and a cup of coffee. Kittens can be visited from about 4 to 8 weeks of age. On arrival, please take your shoes off and wash your hands. Sit down calmly and let the kittens come to you. A visit takes about 45 minutes to an hour; you will meet the mother cat and see how the kittens live. A kitten never goes home right after the first visit.
  4. Going home. If everything feels right, we agree on when your kitten can come home with you. Kittens go home at around 11–12 weeks of age: dewormed, treated for fleas, recently checked by a vet, vaccinated, microchipped, litter-trained, socialised and with a pedigree.
Any questions before requesting a kitten? Feel free to email us at test@viola.nl or call/WhatsApp 06-42291077.
