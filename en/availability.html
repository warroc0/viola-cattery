Availability & placement of British Shorthair kittens

Cattery Viola in Maastricht breeds British Shorthair kittens. We do not offer cat boarding or shelter: this page is about placing our kittens in a new home.

We work with limited places, so kittens are not always available right away.

How does placement work?

  1. Send us an email. Tell us a little about yourself, your home situation and what you are looking for in a kitten.
  2. We get back to you. We’ll let you know whether a kitten is available or when we expect one.
  3. Getting acquainted. We arrange a meeting to get to know each other. [zelf invullen: hoe en waar kennismaking plaatsvindt]
  4. Going home. If everything feels right, we agree on when your kitten can come home with you. [zelf invullen: leeftijd waarop kittens naar huis gaan]
Any questions before requesting a kitten? Feel free to email us at test@viola.nl.
