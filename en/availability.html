How does placement work?

  1. Get in touch. Email, call or message us (06-42291077). Tell us a little about yourself, your home situation and what you are looking for in a kitten.
  2. We get back to you. We’ll let you know whether a kitten is available or when we expect one.
  3. Getting acquainted. We arrange a meeting to get to know each other. [zelf invullen: hoe en waar kennismaking plaatsvindt]
  4. Going home. If everything feels right, we agree on when your kitten can come home with you. Kittens go home from 12 weeks of age: dewormed, treated for fleas, recently checked by a vet, vaccinated, microchipped, litter-trained, socialised and with a pedigree.
Any questions before requesting a kitten? Feel free to email us at test@viola.nl or call/WhatsApp 06-42291077.
