Why a British Shorthair?

Calm character

British Shorthairs are known for being calm, balanced and friendly. They enjoy company without being intrusive.

Beautiful, dense coat

The short, plush coat is characteristic of the breed and needs little grooming.

Great indoors

A British Shorthair adapts well to indoor life and feels at home in a quiet environment.
