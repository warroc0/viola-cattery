Prices for a British Shorthair kitten

At Cattery Viola in Maastricht we keep it simple: there is one price for a British Shorthair kitten.

British Shorthair kitten

€1200

per kitten

What is included?

  • Vaccinated
  • Microchipped
  • Pedigree (international pedigree with a champion line)
  • Dewormed and treated for fleas
  • Recent vet check
  • Litter-trained and socialised
  • Both parents tested for HCM/PKD and FIV/FeLV
  • Ready to go home at around 11–12 weeks
  • Starter pack for the first days at home

Payment

Reservation: €200.

The reservation is deducted from the €1200 price, so after reserving you pay the remaining €1000. We put all agreements in writing together.

You can collect your kitten at around 11–12 weeks, once they are big enough and no longer dependent on their mother. Payment can be made in cash or by bank transfer.

Cattery Viola is a breeder and does not offer boarding or shelter. There are therefore no stay or nightly rates. Questions about the price? Email test@viola.nl or call/WhatsApp 06-42291077.
